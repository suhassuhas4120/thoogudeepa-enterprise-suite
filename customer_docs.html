SECTION 1: Self-Customer Connections & Customer-to-Customer Flow (Screens 1 to 9)

1.1 Table QR Scan Onboarding & Multi-Guest Architecture

When guests arrive at Table T-01 and scan the acrylic table stand QR code with their mobile phone cameras, the customer portal initiates an automated onboarding sequence:

[Customer Scans Table Acrylic QR Stand] │ ▼ https://thoogudeepa-mane.surge.sh/?table=T-01&seat=1 │ ▼ ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐ │ Customer Context Initializes: │ │ • Reads URL parameters: Table = T-01, Seat = 1 │ │ • Subscribes to BroadcastChannel('thoogudeepa_bridge_sync') │ │ • Fetches live menu & out-of-stock (86) inventory │ │ • Opens Screen 1: Welcome & Connect │ └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Multi-Guest / Customer-to-Customer Table Collaboration:

  • Seat Independence: Guest A scans the QR code, lands on Screen 1, and selects Chair C-01. Guest B scans the same QR stand, lands on Screen 1, and selects Chair C-02. Both guests have distinct seat coordinates in their browser state.
  • Order Collaboration & Pooled Bill: Both guests share the unified Table T-01 order ledger. When Guest A adds Biryani and Guest B adds Chicken Ghee Roast, both items append to the shared table order without collision.
  • Bill Splitting Calculation (Screen 6): When proceeding to checkout, either guest can toggle the Split Bill feature. Selecting 2 Diners, 3 Diners, 4 Diners, or 5 Diners calculates the exact equal share:
    Per Person Amount = Math.ceil(Grand Total / Number of Diners)
    Each diner can verify their individual payable share while viewing the consolidated tax breakdown.

Detailed Waiter-to-Customer Updates:

  1. Waiter Taps "Attended" on Assistance Ping:
    The pending call is cleared on the waiter terminal. The customer's Screen 9 status banner updates to: "Captain Suresh attended your request. Thank you!".
  2. Waiter Taps "SERVE" on Ready Dishes:
    When dishes are placed on the dining table, the captain taps SERVE. On the customer's Screen 5, the dish progress bar reaches 100%, and the status badge transitions from Ready to Collected / Served.
  3. Waiter Records Payment in POS:
    The captain confirms receipt of cash or card authorization. Customer Screen 8 unlocks the official Digital Tax Invoice with the verified badge: Attended by Captain Suresh • Console Acknowledged • Seat Released.
  4. Waiter Merges Tables (T-01 + T-02):
    If two parties decide to sit together, the captain merges tables on the tablet. On the customer's smartphone, the header tag updates to T-01 + T-02 (Merged), and the combined dishes reflect on the shared bill.

SECTION 5: Kitchen to Customer Updates

Documents the 4-stage cooking pipeline progression and out-of-stock (86) inventory lockouts.

5.1 The 4-Stage Bump Bar Progression:

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐ │ 4-STAGE KITCHEN BUMP BAR PROGRESSION │ └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘ Stage 1: REC (Received) ──▶ 0% Progress (Gray clock icon on Customer Screen 5) Stage 2: PREP (Preparing) ──▶ 33% Progress (Amber flame icon on Customer Screen 5) Stage 3: READY (Plated) ──▶ 66% Progress (Green pulsing badge; "Mark as Collected" unlocks) Stage 4: SERVED (Collected) ──▶ 100% Progress (Checkmark badge; Dish completed)
  1. Stage 1 — Received (REC): The ticket lands on the chef's KDS screen. On the customer's phone (Screen 5), the item displays a gray clock icon with status Received (0% progress).
  2. Stage 2 — Preparing (PREP): The chef begins cooking and taps PREP. The customer's item card turns amber with a flame icon and status Preparing (33% progress).
  3. Stage 3 — Ready / Plated (READY): The chef garnishes the dish and taps the green READY button on the KDS. The customer's Screen 5 card pulses with a vibrant emerald badge Ready (66% progress), and the tactile "Mark as Collected" button unlocks.
  4. Stage 4 — Served / Collected (COLLECTED): Either the Floor Captain taps SERVE or the customer taps Mark as Collected. The item status updates to Collected / Served (100% progress).

5.2 Kitchen 86 (Sold-Out / Out of Stock) Instant Lockout:

When the head chef runs out of an ingredient (e.g. Mutton Nalli Fry at 02:15 PM):

  • Chef Taps 86 Toggle: On the kitchen tablet (/kitchen), the chef taps the 86 / Sold Out toggle next to that dish.
  • Instant Broadcast: The event is broadcast across BroadcastChannel('thoogudeepa_bridge_sync') with payload { id: 'item-3', is86: true }.
  • Immediate Lockout on Customer Phone:
    • On Screen 2 (Menu): The dish card instantly dims into a disabled state with a bold red badge 86 - Sold Out. The + ADD button is disabled and locked to prevent touches.
    • On Screen 3 (Item Detail): The "Add to Cart" button is disabled and grayed out with a Ban icon.
  • Restoration: As soon as a fresh batch is prepared, the chef untoggles 86 on the KDS, and the dish instantly unlocks across all customer devices.
